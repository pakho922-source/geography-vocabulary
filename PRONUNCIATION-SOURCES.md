# Pronunciation data

American English IPA is derived from [open-dict-data/ipa-dict](https://github.com/open-dict-data/ipa-dict), `data/en_US.txt`, retrieved 2026-10-15. Its MIT license is included in IPA-DICT-LICENSE.txt.

The displayed subset covers 730 distinct reading/glossary entries. Common full-form pronunciations are selected for study; speech may use weak forms. Context-specific homographs and missing compounds, British spelling aliases, possessives and geoscience terms have editorial overrides in the inline wordIPA map. Broad-transcription r/l glyphs are normalized. Mira is a fictional name with a reference pronunciation, not an identified person's verified name pronunciation.

Hydrosphere was cross-checked against https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/hydrosphere .
